Tournament Notes

Quiksilver Cup adds fresh pre-JO evidence.

The 2026 Quiksilver International Water Polo Cup is now captured in CPI as an evidence-intake event across seven age/gender groups. This release updates the data layer and stories, but intentionally does not reorder rankings until alias review is complete.

Data status: Results were readable for 12U Boys, 14U Boys, 14U Girls, 16U Boys, 16U Girls, and 18U Girls. The 18U Boys final and third-place scores were not visible in the text export at intake time, so that group is flagged for final-score review.

What this means for rankings

Quiksilver should matter as a recent pre-JO signal, especially where it adds direct head-to-head results against ranked teams. But CPI should not publish rank movement from this event until team identity is clean. Several teams appeared with short names or alternate labels, including LB Viking, SD Shores, CIU, La Jolla Gold, OVAC, and 680.

The ranking order is therefore unchanged in this release. CPI has added the event as evidence, marked mapped teams with Quiksilver as their latest tournament, and generated a review file for alias cleanup.

Next ranking step

The next pass should review the mapping file, resolve missing teams, confirm the 18U Boys final scores, and then decide which ranking groups warrant movement before JO.
