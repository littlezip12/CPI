Privacy & account security

WPI uses the minimum account data needed to provide secure team access.

← Back to My Teams

What WPI stores

Email address, optional display name, registration and email-verification timestamps, last sign-in timestamp, and the teams you choose to follow. WPI does not store your account password in its public application tables.

Who can see it

Your email is not shown on public score pages. Database Row Level Security limits account records to the signed-in user or authorized team/platform operations.

What WPI does not need

Supporter accounts do not require a phone number, home address, child email address or date of birth. Creating an account does not subscribe you to marketing.

Authentication

Supporters can use one-time email sign-in links instead of a reusable password. Team staff may use password authentication and can add multi-factor authentication as WPI expands privileged-account controls.

Delete your account

Supporters can delete their account from Account Security. Operational Owner/Admin/Scorer accounts must first transfer or remove team responsibilities so game records are not orphaned.
