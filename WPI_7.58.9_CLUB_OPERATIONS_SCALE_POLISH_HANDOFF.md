# WPI 7.58.9 — Club Operations & Scale Polish Handoff

Built from the pushed/validated 7.58.8 baseline. This is still a pre-7.59 pilot release.

### Product changes
- All Teams becomes a scalable operations surface rather than a static card list.
- Owners/Admins can search/filter teams, see readiness at a glance, and jump straight to team Access.
- Club People & Access aggregates memberships and separate Following relationships without changing authority.
- Unlisted manual opponents move into a dedicated identity-review queue; no automatic matching or canonical identity creation occurs.
- Add Team gets a generated clean name/label preview while remaining fully editable.
- Offline/reconnect is intentionally deferred as resilience work. Official 2026–2027 schedule ingestion remains external and should not be fabricated.

### 7.59.0 stance
Do not declare Club Pilot Ready solely because 7.58.9 ships. Real current-season official schedule validation remains externally pending.
