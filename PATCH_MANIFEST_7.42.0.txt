CPI 7.42.0 — Full JO Data Banking & Tournament Evidence
========================================================

Release purpose
---------------
- Give every real tournament team a stable participant identity.
- Preserve tournament-only and out-of-state teams without adding them to rankings automatically.
- Build team-centric tournament evidence and a manual ranking-review queue.
- Surface banked JO schedules/results on CPI team profiles.
- Rebuild evidence after every automated JO source sync.

Current checked-in bootstrap
----------------------------
- 1 banked JO dataset
- 192 normalized games
- 48 stable participant identities
- 39 canonical CPI team matches
- 9 tournament-only identities
- 0 blocking data errors

The GitHub workflow attempts all 23 enabled JO divisions. Run it once manually after deployment to populate every currently reachable source tab.
