- Changes the active scorer heading to **Time of play** and clarifies the single time field as time remaining in the quarter.
- Moves **End quarter** below **Submit play** and reduces its visual weight.
- Keeps **Additional notes** visible by default.

- Keeps starter, roster/setup, message pause, score correction, reset, and End game actions inside Manual controls.
- Makes **End game and build recap** a larger full-width action while retaining progressive disclosure.
- Preserves the complete 7.55.8 reset workflow, team roster, lineups, rankings, tournaments, and historical season data.
