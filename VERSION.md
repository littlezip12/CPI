# WPI 7.53.1

Unified team tournament history.

- Replaces separate JO evidence and historical archive panels with one tournament-history experience for ranked and tournament-only teams.
- Shows tournament dates, division, subdivision, seed, record, placement, source links, and complete game journeys in one expandable event timeline.
- Uses the completed local tournament archive for full historical game-by-game results and attempts to hydrate final Junior Olympics game rows from the WPI relay or official JO sheet, with a safe journey fallback.
- Adds opponent profile links when a canonical WPI team or JO profile is available.
- Preserves all 724 rankings, 182 consolidated clubs, 177 club website links, verified logos, 976 JO placements, scores, identities, and ranking-release data.
