# WPI 7.63.6 — Sponsorship Validation & Event Inventory

WPI 7.63.6 completes the first end-to-end validation path for WPI direct sponsorships without activating outside advertisers or payment collection. Platform Owner can intentionally activate a 24-hour WPI house campaign, pause/reactivate campaigns, and review placement-level impressions/clicks. Free Supporters gain a permanent tournament/weekend results page with event-level sponsor inventory; paid Team Insights / Organization Insights viewers remain ad-free. No third-party programmatic network is enabled and Stripe remains disabled.
