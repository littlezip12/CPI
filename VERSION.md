# WPI 7.63.8 — Free Team Insights Launch Mode

WPI 7.63.8 temporarily unlocks the complete Team Insights experience for authenticated Supporters who follow or belong to a team, with no upgrade CTA required during the launch/adoption period. The future paid subscription and promotional-access infrastructure remains intact but dormant. Free-launch Supporters continue to be ad-supported because free launch access is intentionally separate from paid Team/Organization Insights entitlements. Platform Owner, Owner/Admin/Scorer and explicit paid analytics entitlements remain ad-free under the existing rules.
