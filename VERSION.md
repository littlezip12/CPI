# WPI 7.63.3 — Player Analytics & Supporter Recap Simplification

WPI 7.63.3 deepens Team Insights with player-level analytics that can be filtered to an individual finalized game, a tournament/weekend, or the full selected season. Detailed users can select one player and add up to three teammates for side-by-side comparison across goals, shots, field shooting percentage, per-game production, assists, saves, steals, turnovers, exclusions, 5m events, and tracked shot outcomes. Field shooting percentage is derived from canonical active WPI Live events and keeps shootout results separate.

The free Supporter recap is simplified at the same time: the private Lineups, Player Stats, and Complete Timeline panels no longer render as locked placeholder cards. Supporters keep the free score/period recap plus the single Team Insights upgrade CTA. The detailed panels remain available to Owner/Admin/Scorer and users with Team Insights or Organization Insights access.

Team Insights launch pricing remains **$5/month or $50/year** in preview mode. No Stripe checkout, payment collection, advertisements, Edge Function redeploys, new secrets, scoring-engine changes, GitHub hosting migration, Supabase tier purchase, or scale-tier purchase is enabled in 7.63.3.
