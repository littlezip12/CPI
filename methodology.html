Methodology

How CPI Works

CPI is designed to rank teams based on meaningful, verified tournament competition against the strongest available opponents.

Included Results

Junior Olympics, JO Qualifiers, Futures Super Finals, Futures play where available, and major tournament results.

Excluded Results

Local scrimmages, friendlies, practices, and incomplete local league results are excluded because they are too difficult to verify consistently.

California Polo Index is independent and unofficial. Rankings include verified tournament results only; local scrimmages and friendlies are excluded.
