WPI 7.57.21 — Final Recovery Permission Hotfix
Cumulative hotfix over pushed WPI 7.57.20.

PILOT FIX
- Owner/Admin Reopen Game behavior remains unchanged.
- A Supporter who legitimately accepted a scorer handoff and was the most-recent scorer may now see Reopen game after Final while the 30-minute recovery window is valid.
- Ordinary Supporters who did not score the game do not see the Reopen action.
- The final recap uses a server-side eligibility RPC to decide whether Reopen should be shown; the mutation RPC remains the final authority.
- Repeated reopen/final cycles remain supported, including a Supporter whose next scorer session was created by game_reopen.

RELIABILITY PRESERVED
- 7.57.20 handoff delivery fix remains intact.
- 7.57.19 score preservation, immediate control recovery, repeat Final Whistle, and fresh full recap behavior remain intact.
- No Edge Function changes.
- No secret changes.
