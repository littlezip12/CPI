# CPI 7.52.7 — JO Results Logos and Journey Links

This cumulative release includes the 7.52.6 club-logo expansion plus the San Diego Shores logo and JO results enhancements.

## Scope

- Club logos appear beside every team in the 2026 JO results browser.
- Clicking a team opens the correct Boys or Girls/Coed JO viewer, division, and team journey.
- The selected journey shows the team’s completed games, scores, opponents, venues, and bracket path.
- Homepage JO result previews also show logos and link to the selected team journey.
- Deep links match team names without requiring identical capitalization or punctuation.
- San Diego Shores uses the newly supplied logo everywhere.
- Ranking order, CPI values, team labels, and tournament placements remain unchanged.

## Validation

Run `./release-check` after applying the patch. Do not commit or push unless the final line is:
