Tournament Notes · Release 7.30 v2

Quiksilver Cup adds movement, but not a ranking reset.

WPI now includes Quiksilver Cup results as a secondary pre-JO signal. Because fields ranged from only 5 to 18 teams, the event is weighted below JO, Futures Super Finals, US Club Championships, JO Qualifiers, and larger invitationals.

What changed in 7.30 v2

The first 7.30 draft moved some teams too aggressively for a limited-field tournament. This revised release caps Quiksilver movement by field size and protects teams that did not play from heavy penalties.

  • 5–6 team fields are treated as a light signal, mostly producing small bumps or review flags.
  • 12 team fields can move winners/finalists modestly, but not like a major championship.
  • 16–18 team fields carry the strongest Quiksilver weight, but still below major season anchors.
  • 18U Boys remains guarded because final/third-place source score confirmation is still open.

How WPI weighted it

Quiksilver sits around 0.22–0.40 depending on age-group field size. Junior Olympics remains the 1.00 anchor, with Futures Super Finals, US Club Championships, JO Qualifiers, and large-field invitationals all carrying more weight.
