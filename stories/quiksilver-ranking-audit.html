Ranking QA

Quiksilver ranking movement was capped by field size.

Release 7.30 v2 treats Quiksilver as secondary evidence because each age group had a limited field.

Quiksilver results are included in CPI, but they do not carry the same weight as Junior Olympics, Futures, US Club Championships, JO Qualifiers, or larger field events.

The audit file documents old rank, new rank, movement, field size, event weight, movement cap notes, and review flags for the affected teams.
