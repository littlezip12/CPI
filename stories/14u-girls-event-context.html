Rankings Note

14U Girls shows why event context matters

The current 14U Girls rankings combine multiple major-event signals. That makes context more important than simply sorting by one tournament finish or one recent record.

14U GirlsPre-JO snapshotEvent context

Why this group is tricky

14U Girls has high-quality signals from both Futures Super Finals and US Club Championships. CPI treats those as peer major-event inputs rather than assuming one automatically overrides the other.

Current top-line picture

In the current Pre-JO snapshot, Patriot Navy, 908, Stanford A, Lamorinda A, and San Diego Shores Black sit inside the top five. The ordering reflects results, event quality, opponent paths, and connected comparison points.

Why records alone are not enough

A clean record in one path is not always equal to a mixed record in a deeper path. CPI looks at who a team played, where the result came from, and whether there are common opponents that connect otherwise separate brackets.

What to watch after JO

Junior Olympics will be the strongest validation point. Teams that outperform their Pre-JO position will force ranking movement, but CPI will still consider the full season body of work and not only the final placement line.

The goal is to compare like with like: event strength, opponent quality, bracket path, and team identity all have to be understood together.
