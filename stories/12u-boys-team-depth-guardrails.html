Ranking System

Why team-depth guardrails matter

A/B/C/D team labels matter because a lower-depth team can be strong without being the same ranking entity as the club’s top team.

Team identity12U Boys pilotAlias cleanup

Why this matters

Youth water polo rankings can get messy when A, B, C, and mixed entries are merged too aggressively. CPI treats team-depth as an important identity signal, not just a naming detail.

How CPI handles it

A B team can rank above lower A teams if the results support it, but CPI avoids merging B-team evidence into an A-team profile unless the source data clearly indicates the same team.

Where this showed up first

The 12U Boys ranking pilot forced several team-depth decisions because teams had overlapping club names, mixed coed context, and different tournament paths. That pilot became the template for later ranking groups.

What the manual audit will fix

The upcoming team audit will identify duplicate entries, wrong aliases, incorrect club mappings, and places where a team name should be normalized without losing roster-depth context.

The objective is simple: clean team names for readers, but precise team identity behind the scenes.
