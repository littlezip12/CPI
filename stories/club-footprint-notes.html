Club pages now focus on footprint

Club profiles are being cleaned up so they describe the club, not one team’s weekend. The goal is a clearer view of club depth across age groups.

Club profilesRegion cleanupUI cleanup

Why the club page changed

Earlier club pages mixed club-level information with team-specific movement and best-win snippets. That was confusing because a club can have several teams across ages, genders, and roster-depth levels.

What belongs on a club page

A club page should focus on ranked teams, best ranked team, average WPI, region, website, and age-group footprint. Team movement, latest result, and best win belong on the team page where age, gender, and roster context are clear.

How regions now work

WPI now uses a simplified region model: San Diego, Orange County, Los Angeles, Inland Empire, Central Coast, Central Valley, Sacramento, East Bay, Peninsula / San Francisco, Out of State, and Needs Review.

What still needs review

The next cleanup layer is manual team audit work: duplicate teams, aliases, wrong club mappings, missing logos, and team-depth assumptions. Those fixes will make club profiles more accurate over time.

Club pages should answer one question quickly: where does this club show up across the WPI landscape?
