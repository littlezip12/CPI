# CPI Release 7.30 v2 — Quiksilver Lightweight Ranking Adjustments

## Why v2 exists

The first 7.30 draft weighted Quiksilver too heavily for a tournament with only 5–18 teams per age group. This version includes Quiksilver evidence but caps movement based on field size.

## Weighting model

- Junior Olympics: 1.00
- Futures Super Finals / US Club Championships: 0.75–0.85
- JO Qualifiers: 0.60–0.75
- Major large-field invitationals: 0.50–0.65
- Quiksilver Cup: 0.22–0.40 depending on field size

## Guardrails

- Small fields create small bumps or review flags.
- Non-participating teams are not heavily penalized.
- Poor Quiksilver finishes can flag review but generally do not create major drops.
- 18U Boys remains guarded because final/third-place source scores need confirmation.
- New team entries are placed conservatively and remain review-sensitive.
