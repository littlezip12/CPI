# WPI 7.53.3 — Navigation and Homepage Clarity

## Public experience

- Stories is retired from the universal header, mobile navigation, quick links, footer, homepage, subscribe page, QA links, and tournament templates.
- The preserved `stories/` archive remains in the repository. Requests to `stories.html` redirect to the homepage product guide.
- The universal navigation now prioritizes Home, Rankings, Teams, Clubs, Tournaments, and Methodology.
- The header search action routes directly to the homepage team finder and focuses team search when already on the homepage.

## Homepage

- The hero explains WPI as a connected rankings, teams, clubs, and tournament-results platform.
- Four primary pathways lead directly to Rankings, Team Search, Clubs, and Tournaments.
- The former story/update module is replaced by a stable platform guide.
- Boys and girls post-Junior Olympics ranking context remains explicit.

## Data protection

This release changes navigation and presentation only. It preserves 724 rankings, 182 clubs, 177 club website links, 976 JO placements, all identity/logo corrections, the sponsor framework, and unified tournament histories.
