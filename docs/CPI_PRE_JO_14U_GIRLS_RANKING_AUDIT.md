## Ranking model notes

- Futures Super Finals and US Club Championships are weighted as peer major events.
- Teams playing both events receive higher confidence, not an automatic ranking bump.
- Common-opponent crossover is used when teams do not share the same major event.
- Platinum/D1 anchors the top bands; Gold/D2 and Silver/D3 movement requires supporting evidence.
- A/B/C team depth is respected.
- Same-team naming variants are consolidated before publication.
