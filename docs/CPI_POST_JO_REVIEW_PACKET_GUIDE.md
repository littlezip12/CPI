# CPI Post-JO Review Packet Guide

## Purpose

Review packets are the human QA layer between raw JO results and published CPI rankings.

The packet should make the ranking decision transparent enough that a reviewer can answer:

- Why did this team move up?
- Why did this team move down?
- Did JO confirm or contradict the pre-JO CPI model?
- Are we accidentally merging different roster identities?
- Are we overreacting to one game or underreacting to a major JO result?

## Packet sections

### 1. Group summary

Include:

- Age/gender group.
- Number of ranked teams reviewed.
- JO divisions included.
- Source completion status.
- Data confidence.
- Major caveats.

### 2. Pre-JO top 25

For each team:

- Pre-JO rank.
- CPI score.
- Club.
- Team designation.
- Best pre-JO evidence.
- Known flags.

### 3. JO finish summary

For each team:

- JO division.
- Final placement.
- Record.
- Placement game.
- Best win.
- Worst loss.
- Bracket path note.

### 4. Overperformers

A team should be flagged as an overperformer when it materially exceeds pre-JO CPI expectations.

Possible triggers:

- Finishes 8+ ranking positions better than expected.
- Beats two or more teams ranked above it.
- Wins a division while ranked outside the expected top tier.
- Reaches a top placement from a lower pre-JO rank.

### 5. Underperformers

A team should be flagged as an underperformer when it materially trails pre-JO CPI expectations.

Possible triggers:

- Finishes 8+ ranking positions worse than expected.
- Loses multiple games to teams ranked below it.
- Drops into a lower placement bracket than expected.
- Has a poor JO result that conflicts with a high pre-JO CPI score.

### 6. Alias review

Check:

- Same club with multiple teams in the same age/gender group.
- Color/team designations.
- A/B/C identities.
- Source names that changed across tournaments.
- Teams that could be mislabeled across boys/girls or adjacent ages.

### 7. Team-depth review

Check whether a club's B/C teams are being ranked appropriately.

General guidance:

- B teams can outrank weaker A teams with direct evidence.
- B teams should not be automatically tied to A-team performance.
- C teams need clear game-level evidence to jump B teams.
- Strong club depth is a signal, but it cannot replace team-specific results.

### 8. Recommended post-JO changes

For each proposed move:

- Current rank.
- Proposed rank.
- Reason.
- Evidence.
- Confidence.
- Reviewer note.

### 9. Final QA checks

Before publishing:

- [ ] Every top-25 team has a JO result or confirmed absence note.
- [ ] Every major JO top finisher is represented.
- [ ] Alias corrections are documented.
- [ ] Team-depth decisions are documented.
- [ ] Major overperformers/underperformers are reviewed.
- [ ] No cross-age or cross-gender aliases were merged by mistake.
- [ ] Final rank order is internally consistent.
- [ ] Snapshot manifest is updated.
