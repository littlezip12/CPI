# How CPIRE Works

**CPIRE** stands for **CPI Rating Engine**.

CPIRE estimates team strength. It is not a trophy count, tournament-finish list, or popularity ranking.

## What CPIRE measures

CPIRE asks:

> If two teams played tomorrow on a neutral site, who is more likely to win?

## Core principles

1. Every game is evidence.
2. Strong opponents matter.
3. Close losses to elite teams can still be useful evidence.
4. Tournament path matters more than final placement alone.
5. Goal differential is capped.
6. More connected data means higher confidence.
7. A team's resume explains the ranking but does not replace the rating model.

## Confidence

Confidence measures certainty, not quality.

A team can be excellent but still have lower confidence if it has limited games or limited cross-regional opponents.
