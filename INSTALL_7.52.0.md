# CPI 7.52.0 — Boys Post-JO Rankings

This release publishes the approved 2026 post-Junior Olympics Boys rankings.

## Scope

- Publishes top-100 rankings for 12U, 14U, 16U, and 18U Boys.
- Preserves the approved JO-derived ordering and reviewed one-position adjustments.
- Keeps deserving B/C teams at their earned positions.
- Normalizes club colors to functional A/B/C/D team depth.
- Consolidates Shore Aquatics and Long Beach Shore under one club identity.
- Normalizes North Irvine without the source-only "Vegas" prefix.
- Maps Kern Premier to SKIP where only one related entry exists.
- Leaves all Girls ranking positions unchanged.
