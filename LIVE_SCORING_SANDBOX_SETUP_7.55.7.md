# WPI Live Scoring Sandbox — 7.55.7

## Pilot team
The local pilot defaults to **Lamorinda A 14U Boys**. The roster heading follows the selected team name so the same interface can be reused by another team later.

## Roster
Use **Add player** to insert a new row. The page scrolls to the row and focuses the cap field. Enter both cap number and player name before selecting a lineup.

## Play entry
During a game, keep quarter and time current, select the play and player, choose an assist only for goals, optionally add a note, and press **Submit play**. The play records immediately. Play/player/assist/note reset; quarter/time remain.

## Quarter transition
Press **End quarter**. WPI records the end-of-quarter score at 0:00 and immediately opens the next-quarter lineup. Scoring cannot resume until the required goalie and field starters are saved.
