# Water Polo Index (WPI)

WPI is an independent youth water polo rankings, club, team, and tournament platform.

## Competitive-season convention

Public history uses full water polo season ranges rather than calendar years:

- `2025–2026` begins with fall 2025 tournaments and closes after the summer 2026 championship cycle.
- `2026–2027` begins with fall 2026 tournaments and closes after the summer 2027 championship cycle.

Each tournament retains its actual event year and dates separately from its competitive-season assignment.
