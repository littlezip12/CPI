# CPI 5.1 Design System

## Direction

CPI is moving from a dark dashboard look to a lighter sports publication look.

## Core principles

- Light background.
- White cards.
- Navy header and accents.
- Blue for rankings/actions.
- Gold for champions/features.
- Green for positive movement.
- Red for negative movement.
- More whitespace.
- Consistent card styling.
- Consistent hover states.

## Tokens

- Primary Navy: `#071426`
- CPI Blue: `#126dff`
- CPI Gold: `#f6b700`
- Positive Green: `#16a34a`
- Page Background: `#f4f7fb`
- Card Background: `#ffffff`

## Components included

- Header/nav
- Cards/panels
- Buttons
- Badges/kickers
- Ranking rows
- Club rows
- Story cards
- Ticker
- Footer
