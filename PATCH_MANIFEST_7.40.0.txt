CPI 7.40.0 — Canonical Club and Team Identity Foundation

PURPOSE
Create one stable identity layer shared by rankings, club records, JO schedule markup, and the ranking-engine migration.

KEY RESULTS
- 138 canonical clubs produced from 143 legacy club records.
- 506 stable season/age/gender team identities.
- 1,078 scope-safe team aliases and 598 globally unique fallback aliases.
- Five duplicate legacy club families converge on shared canonical IDs.
- Ranking order, team names, club names, and legacy public URLs remain unchanged.
- Boys and Girls JO matchup markup now carries canonical team/club IDs when a ranked identity resolves.
- Identity validation is included in ./release-check.
